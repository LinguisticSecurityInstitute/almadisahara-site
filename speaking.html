Short bio

Sahara Al-Madi is a computational linguist and the founder of the Linguistic Security Institute, an open-source research initiative holding AI accountable to the communities whose languages it processes. Her work spans multilingual NLP, AI security, governance and Indigenous data sovereignty.

Full bio

Sahara Al-Madi is a computational linguist working at the intersection of language, AI security and governance. She founded the Linguistic Security Institute (LSI), an open-source research initiative built on a simple principle: AI systems must be accountable to the communities whose languages they process. Her research on Arabic NLP has been published at ACL 2026 (SemEval) and earned first place in the BAREC 2026 Constrained Track. She has presented at BSides San Diego, the Geological Society of America, the GEO Indigenous Summit and DEATHCon, and builds open-source tools including the Linguistic Firewall and Seven Generations in Orbit. Sahara has led multilingual AI data and evaluation work in industry, holds a B.A. in Applied Linguistics from UCLA, and is pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at Georgia Tech. She works in English and Spanish, with research in Arabic.
