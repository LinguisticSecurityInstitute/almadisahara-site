Areas of work

A linguist building systems that are secure, accountable, and educational for the communities they serve.

My work asks not only whether a system works, but for whom it works, whom it fails, and who gets to decide — across computational linguistics, cybersecurity, AI governance, conservation and space policy.

Linguistic security

How language itself becomes an attack surface: polyglot prompt injection, code-switching (Spanglish, Arabizi) and dialect gaps in AI guardrails.

AI governance

Risk assessment frameworks and accountability protocols for language integrity across the AI lifecycle.

Indigenous data sovereignty

Community consent and control over data about their languages, lands and knowledge — from archives to Earth observation.

Multilingual NLP & evaluation

LLM evaluation, error analysis and golden-label quality for Arabic, Spanish and dialectal variation.

Language revitalization

Tools for low-resource and endangered languages, like Mitote, a Nahuatl-aware text-to-speech prototype.

Earth observation & space

Data ethics for geoscience and conservation, and long-horizon thinking for responsible space-debris prioritization.

Demos

Featured demos

Working prototypes from the Linguistic Security Institute, open on GitHub.

AI security · BSides San Diego 2026

Linguistic Firewall: Polyglot Poisoning

A prototype that probes LLM guardrails for "polyglot poisoning": using dialects, scripts and code-switching to slip past safety filters tuned mostly for English.

  • Web interface and API on Python 3.9+
  • Runs against local Ollama-compatible models
  • Basis for the DEATHCon 2026 hands-on workshop

Responsible disclosure: full attack prompts are shared only with verified researchers working on defenses.

YOHAKU Space Debris Hackathon · Challenge 2

Seven Generations in Orbit

A decision tool for choosing which space-debris objects to take responsibility for first, built on seven questions grounded in the Lakota principles shared through the challenge.

  • Six real debris objects, each with its web of relationships
  • Compares today’s ranking with one seven generations ahead
  • Refuses to rank until standing to decide is established
